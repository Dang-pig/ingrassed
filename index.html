I’ve always felt a deep sense of connection with nature

Before we had cities, concrete roads, and machinery infused in our lives, we were already part of nature.
“Our bodies and souls cycle along with her. Birth, life, and death. Ingestion, digestion, excretion. Light and dark. Tranquility and turmoil. Inhaling and exhaling. Receiving and giving”.

Indeed, human lives are inherently ingrained in nature.

That is where INGRASSED began. Inspired by the word “ingrained” and rooted in grass, something so simple yet fundamental, Ingrassed is my way of rethinking one of the most everyday items that wields so much influence on the environment: the straw. As a common type of single-use plastic and then thrown away, straws are one of the culprits of plastic pollution. But there is a solution to everything, isn’t there?

And Nature is the key. By switching to a biodegradable alternative such as grass, we hope to not only tackle plastic pollution that has been prevalent in Hanoi and Vietnam for decades, but also bring a little more of nature back into our life of hustle bustle.

FROM F&B TO SCHOOL CAFETERIA

One of my project’s proudest achievements is having contributed to a positive change in my beloved high school, even if that transformation began with something as small as grass straws.

After a trial period using Green Joy grass straws introduced through Ingrassed, the Cafe Book cafeteria at Hanoi - Amsterdam High School for the Gifted, or the most popular student hangout on campus, received enthusiastic feedback, and gradually they came to recognize the immense benefits these straws offer for both the environment as well as an individual’s health. As a result, grass straws are now officially in use at Cafe Book!

The students were a little surprised to witness the shift at first, but as I interview them now, they enjoy grass straws significantly more than the plastic ones. Even more than other green alternative options.

“They’re really durable, way more than paper straws. I did bite through them the first time I used such straws, but we can all control that habit.” - One student shared.

“I like that grass straws don’t leave a strong aftertaste like bamboo straws, another eco-friendly alternative I’ve used a lot. Instead, they enhance the refreshing, subtle quality of the drink.” - Another expressed their opinion.

“For the past years, we’ve only been utilizing plastic straws because our budget couldn’t afford any further. I admit, sometimes sipping the drinks myself, I’m put off by the “taste of plastic”. Not that they give off this hose-like, rubbery note; we only stock high-quality ones. But because of that exact neutral taste, they feel a little bit “weird” and artificial. You know what they often say? “If nature doesn’t make it, don’t use it.” All the accumulated concerns we once felt when using plastic ones seemed to vanish with the arrival of grass straws. Our cafeteria is so delighted to have Anh Thu introduce this green alternative that is both reasonably priced while also providing a positive experience for any user.” - Mrs. Mai, the manager of Cafe Book, concluded sincerely.

This has been a successful approach that reaffirms my belief since I first took environmental initiatives as a middle school student: Change begins within one’s own community. Before aspiring to transform society, I want to improve my surroundings first, including my beloved school I’ve been with for 7 years. It might seem modest, but I believe with every pioneering school, such as Hanoi - Amsterdam, setting an example by shift towards green consumption, the day will soon come when all of this becomes the norm.

ROLLING BEANS COFFEE'S THOUGHTS ON GRASS STRAWS

After a trial period using Green Joy grass straws introduced through Ingrassed, the Cafe Book cafeteria at Hanoi - Amsterdam High School for the Gifted, or the most popular student hangout on campus, received enthusiastic feedback, and gradually they came to recognize the immense benefits these straws offer for both the environment as well as an individual’s health. As a result, grass straws are now officially in use at Cafe Book!

1. How is switching to grass straws meaningful for Rolling Beans Coffee?
From the very beginning, Rolling Beans has been committed to sustainability. Every piece of our interior décor is crafted from old boat wood. Therefore, switching to grass straws feels like the next significant step toward our goal of becoming a truly "green" café. While this change is not some extraordinary, we believe that every small step plays a vital role in fostering long-term sustainable habits.

2. What advantages and benefits does the use of grass straws bring to the shop?
The biggest plus is that grss straws are incredibly sturdy; they do not disintegrate or turn mushy after prolonged soaking like paper straws do, thereby preserving the drink's true flavor. While some people have remarked that the straws are somewhat brittle and prone to snapping when bitten, our own testing has shown that, as long as they are stored and used correctly, they can remain intact and durable for hours. Furthermore, their rustic, soothing aesthetic complements the shop's wooden interior perfectly, creating a delightful experience that appeals to both the eyes and the palate.

3. How have customers responded to this type of straw?
The positive feedback has truly exceeded our expectations! Young people and international tourists, in particular, are thrilled to experience this for the first time. There’s something really satisfying about becoming a "green warrior”, you know. We’ve also received great praise for the thoughtful touches at the shop; many customers even ask about the origin of these straws. For me, that enthusiastic support is a huge source of motivation that assures us in the path we’ve chosen.

4. Did Rolling Beans Coffee face any difficulties or inconveniences during the transition towards grass straws?
To be honest, the initial cost is noticeably higher than plastic straws, and being a natural product, a grass straw requires extra care to prevent humidity and mold. However, seeing our customers' warm support as well as the long-term environmental benefits, we believe this investment is totally worth it.

5. Thank you for sharing your thoughts so sincerely. Finally, I’d like to ask: do you have any message for other F&B businesses that are considering a switch to eco-friendly products?
Honestly, we understand that making the initial change often brings concerns regarding costs, customer experience, and so on. However, in today’s increasingly modern and progressive society, young people are well aware of the importance of environmental protection as well as sustainable development. I believe that in the future, green products will become the norm. So, if your budget allows, take a long-term view and start making changes, even the smallest ones.

HOW TO STORE GRASS STRAWS? ANSWERS TO SOME FAQs

Grass straw is simple to use! Just insert it into your cup and start sipping! Beyond usage, proper storage is also crucial. Here are the recommendations from manufacturers of standard and safe methods for storing grass straws at home:

FOR DRIED GRASS STRAWS:

  • Store in a clean, dry place to prevent bacterial contamination.
  • Keep them in paper bags, paper boxes, or airtight containers to protect against dust and insects.
  • Avoid humid environments and direct sunlight.
  • Refrigeration is recommended to maintain optimal product quality. The shelf life is typically up to 12 months (at temperatures of 10–30°C and humidity below 65%).
  • Do not wash the straws with dish soap or chemical detergents to ensure user safety.

AS FOR FRESH GRASS STRAWS:

  • They last about 5 days at room temperature (approx. 27–28°C) and 2 weeks to 3 months in the refrigerator.
  • They can be stored for up to 6 months in the freezer; simply thaw them before use or use them directly in cold drinks.

Next, let’s move on to the Q&A section. Here is some important information to keep in mind when using grass straws:

1. HOW LONG CAN THE STRAW SOAK IN LIQUID?

Grass straws can retain their structural integrity and firmness for up to 4 hours in a drink as they are relatively durable.

2. CAN GRASS STRAWS BE REUSED?

For personal use, you can wash the straw with water, air-dry it, and store it in an airtight container to reuse it 5–7 times. However, for a café or restaurant, it is best used once to ensure hygiene.

3. DO GRASS STRAWS AFFECT THE TASTE OR AROMA OF THE DRINK?

Made 100% from grey sedge, these straws are tasteless but carry a subtle, natural scent, offering a unique and enjoyable experience with your drink.

4. CAN THEY BE USED WITH HOT DRINKS?

Yes, they are suitable for hot, warm, and cold drinks. However, avoid using them with extremely hot liquids or boiling water, as this can compromise the straw's durability.

Given their sustainability and versatility, grass straws show great promise as a widely popular "green" solution for the future, helping us create a greener and cleaner environment for generations to come.

GREEN JOY - MY COMPANION

While deep diving into grass straws, I discovered a powerful source of motivation that made me optimistic about their potential and future: Green Joy.

Green Joy Co., Ltd. is a pioneering Vietnamese enterprise founded in 2018 by Ms. Vo Quoc Thao Nguyen, specializing in the production and sale of grass straws made from grey sedges.

Green Joy’s story began with a video showing a plastic straw stuck in a sea turtle's nostril and beaches along Vietnam's central coast littered with trash. Deeply troubled by these scenes, Ms. Nguyen felt compelled to take action to transform the beaches of her homeland. It was the surrounding nature itself that led her to the idea of ​​grass straws.

Grey sedge is a plant long familiar the Mekong Delta and Vietnamese people in general, yet people were gradually forgetting about it. Furthermore, residents of the Mekong Delta face significant challenges from climate change, such as drought and saltwater intrusion, resulting in unstable jobs and low incomes. Green Joy was thus established with a noble mission: not only to help preserve our planet but also to raise consumer awareness, inspire others, promote the value of nature and ultimately contribute a sustainable business solution to the community.

Throughout its eight-year journey, Green Joy has made a significant positive impact on both the environment and the socio-economic landscape. According to the company's official website, Green Joy has helped eliminate 5 million plastic straws and replace 18 million kilograms of plastic annually, while also improving the livelihoods of over 1,000 farmers and local residents in Long An province and the Mekong Delta. Notably, the brand has boasted an extensive reach, currently present in hundreds of restaurants and hotels and exporting to over 30 countries like the US, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, and South Korea.

Beyond these achievements, a standout moment was the brand's appearance in the famous Shark Tank Vietnam, a business reality show, in 2019: it sparked a bidding war among the "sharks" and set a record for the fastest start-up to secure a deal on the show. In 2021, Green Joy was also honored to receive the International Innovators Award (IIA). Subsequently, the brand once again made its mark on the startup scene by winning the championship of VietChallenge 2021 with a cash prize of $15,000.

Despite this impressive track record, Green Joy is not slowing down. With the goal of becoming the nation's leading and a globally trusted supplier of grass straws, Ms. Nguyen Vo and her brand are poised to deliver even greater values in the future.

THE DOWNSIDES OF OVERUSING GRASS STRAWS?

Grass straws are made from a natural material that is fully biodegradable within a few days to a few weeks, seemingly an unbeatable green alternative to plastic straws. However, before adopting them on a massive scale, we must fully understand the downsides and consequences of overusing this type of straw!

First, such behavior impacts natural resources themselves. It takes six months to a year for sedge plants to grow, mature and reach the standard height required for making straws or weaving. This is a significant amount of time; if demand for these straws suddenly surges, the material supply may not regenerate fast enough to keep pace. This leads to over-harvesting and places strain on land, water sources, and the ecosystems of regions like the Southwestern Mekong Delta.

Mass production also increases the costs and energy required for preliminary processing, drying, and short-term storage. Furthermore, regarding straws made from natural, biodegradable materials, some consumers mistakenly believe that it’s acceptable to toss them on the street or into bushes. While it is true that they decompose, they do not do so instantly; “littering” only creates large amounts of organic waste, marring urban aesthetics as well as negatively affecting public sanitation.

Ultimately, the most sustainable solution is to minimize straw usage when it is not necessary, rather than misunderstanding the concept and overusing "green" materials. Personally, I’d like to see F&B shops - as shops must ensure a complete experience for customes - replace plastic with more eco-friendly alternatives. However, when an individual customer buys a drink, it is advisable to simply cut back on straw or bring along a reusable one.

GRASS STRAWS - A WONDERFUL “GREEN ALTERNATIVE”

As the name suggests, these straws are made from the hollow stems of grass, specifically grey sedge plant, primarily grown in the Mekong Delta, and come in two main varieties: fresh and dried.

Fresh grass straws are green and have a shelf life of 1–3 days at room temperature or 2–4 weeks in the refrigerator. Dried grass straws are light brown or natural green and can be stored for 6–12 months at room temperature or up to a year in the refrigerator.

With a standard length of ~20 cm and a diameter from 4.5 mm to 7 mm, they are ideal for drinking water, tea, coffee, or thin smoothies. Cultivating sedge plants isn’t overly labor-intensive for farmers; after harvesting by cutting the stems close to the base, the plant regrows from the existing rootstock without needing to be replanted from scratch. A single crop area can yield a stable harvest for 5–7 years before plants need to be replaced.

Regarding advantages, this is a 100% natural product; the processes of cutting, cleaning the interior, and drying involve no chemicals or preservatives. Additionally, grass straws are biodegradable, decomposing in soil within just ~30 days (according to various studies), enriching soil with organic matter rather than polluting the environment. Finally, grass straws’ standount feature is their ability to remain sturdy in liquid for hours without disintegrating, unlike some other eco-friendly straw alternatives. They are moderately priced, costing less than bamboo or rice straws.

There are, however, some limitations; due to the plant’s nature, the straw's diameter is not wide enough for beverages containing toppings like tapioca pearls or jelly. However, one must be careful not to bite the straw as it’s somewhat brittle. Nevertheless, if you prioritize a product that is completely natural, free from harmful substances, absolutely safe and environmentally friendly, grass straw is an excellent choice.
